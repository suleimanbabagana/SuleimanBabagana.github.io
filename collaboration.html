International Research Trajectory

International engagement provides an important foundation for the next phase of comparative Military Performance Management research.

From International Academic Engagement to Comparative MPM Research

International academic engagement has included research and conference participation in Malaysia, the United Kingdom, Brunei and Ghana. In 2021, a formal post-doctoral research fellowship offer from Universiti Pertahanan Nasional Malaysia (UPNM) proposed research on Understanding Military Performance Management in Context: Applicability in the Nigerian Army. Although the fellowship was not taken up at the time, the research theme continued to develop through publications, conference presentations, conceptual work and institutional development.

Current collaborative ambitions extend this trajectory toward comparative and multi-institutional research capable of testing, refining and applying Military Performance Management across different organisational and national contexts.

This section records academic engagement and research trajectory. It does not represent exploratory discussions or prospective institutions as concluded partnerships.

Principles for Collaboration

Effective collaboration should create academic and institutional value while maintaining clear governance and scholarly standards.

Mutual Academic Value

Partnerships should contribute substantive expertise, comparative insight, methodological strength or institutional relevance to the research.

Integrity & Institutional Approval

Research should respect academic independence, ethical requirements, institutional authority, confidentiality and applicable approval processes.

Clear & Sustainable Outputs

Collaborative work should define responsibilities and pursue credible outputs such as research publications, policy briefs, frameworks, professional learning and longer-term research networks.
