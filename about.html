Academic Profile

Management Scholarship, Performance and Institutional Effectiveness

I am a Management scholar at Nigerian Army University Biu, Nigeria. My academic work spans human resource management, organisational behaviour, performance management, strategic performance management and institutional effectiveness. Across these areas, my scholarship is concerned with how people, organisational systems and strategy interact to shape performance and institutional outcomes.

Building on this broader Management foundation, my current research programme focuses particularly on Military Performance Management (MPM)—its conceptual and theoretical foundations and its application to military and defence institutions. My work examines how the performance of military personnel, units and institutions connects with organisational effectiveness, military capability, accountability and the achievement of strategic defence objectives.

This research direction brings together perspectives from performance management, strategic management, human resource management, organisational studies, public-sector management, security studies and defence management. It treats military performance as a multi-level phenomenon extending beyond individual appraisal to the systematic alignment and improvement of performance across personnel, units and institutions.

Alongside research and teaching, I contribute to academic leadership and institutional development at Nigerian Army University Biu. My current responsibilities include coordinating the Centre for Military Performance Management and directing the Directorate of Basic and Pre-Degree Programmes, while also contributing to postgraduate research development and supervision.

Scholarly Direction

Research, Teaching and Institutional Engagement

My academic work combines established Management scholarship with a developing programme of research focused on performance in military and defence institutions. These activities are connected through three complementary dimensions.

Research

Research on human resource and performance-management systems, organisational processes, strategic alignment and institutional effectiveness, with Military Performance Management representing a distinctive and growing area of scholarship.

Teaching & Research Development

Teaching and supervision across Management and related fields, with emphasis on connecting theory, rigorous research and contextually relevant organisational problems.

Institutional Engagement

Academic leadership, research development, policy engagement and capacity development through university responsibilities and the Centre for Military Performance Management.
