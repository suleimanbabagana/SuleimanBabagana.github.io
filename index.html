Current Research Programme

Military Performance Management

A major focus of my current scholarship is Military Performance Management (MPM), an emerging interdisciplinary area connecting performance management, strategic management, human resource management and defence-management perspectives.

This research approaches military performance as a multi-level phenomenon extending beyond individual appraisal to the management, alignment and improvement of performance across personnel, units, institutions, military capability and strategic defence objectives.

Individual Military Performance → Unit Performance → Institutional Performance → Military Capability → Strategic Defence Performance

The programme further examines the roles of leadership, human resources, training, doctrine, organisational systems, resources, technology, accountability and institutional adaptation in supporting military and strategic objectives.
