Current Research Programme

Military Performance Management

A major focus of my current scholarship is Military Performance Management (MPM), an emerging interdisciplinary area connecting performance management, strategic management, human resource management, organisational studies, public-sector management, security studies and defence-management perspectives.

MPM examines military performance as a multi-level phenomenon extending beyond individual appraisal to the systematic management, alignment and improvement of performance across personnel, units and institutions, and ultimately to military capability, institutional effectiveness and the achievement of strategic defence and security objectives.

Individual Military Performance → Unit Performance → Institutional Performance → Military Capability → Strategic Defence Performance

The research programme examines how leadership, human resources, training, doctrine, organisational systems, resources, technology, accountability, institutional adaptation and strategic alignment interact to shape military performance and effectiveness. It also investigates how performance-management theories, frameworks and practices can be adapted to the distinctive organisational, operational and institutional characteristics of military and defence organisations.

The programme provides a platform for conceptual and empirical research, comparative and cross-institutional studies, postgraduate research, executive and professional education, policy engagement, institutional capacity development and interdisciplinary collaboration. It offers opportunities for partnerships involving military, defence and security institutions, universities and research institutes, government and public-sector agencies, development partners and international organisations interested in performance, capability development, institutional effectiveness, security-sector governance and strategic outcomes.
