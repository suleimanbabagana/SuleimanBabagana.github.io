Suleiman Abubakar Babagana, PhD

Management Scholar | Military Performance Management (MPM) | Strategic Performance & Defence Management
Reader (Associate Professor) of Management
Nigerian Army University Biu, Nigeria
Coordinator, Centre for Military Performance Management (CMPM)

My scholarship focuses on the application of management and performance-management principles to organisations and institutions, with an emerging specialization in Military Performance Management (MPM). My research examines how individual, unit and institutional performance can be aligned with organisational capabilities and strategic objectives, with particular attention to military and defence institutions.
